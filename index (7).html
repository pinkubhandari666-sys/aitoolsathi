Create GST and Standard Invoices Online for Free

AI Tool Saathi Invoice Maker helps freelancers, shop owners and small businesses prepare a clear invoice online. Choose a template, enter seller and customer details, add products or services, apply discounts and calculate GST. You can print the finished invoice or save it as a PDF using your browser.

Features of the Free Invoice Maker

  • Create GST invoices with CGST, SGST or IGST calculations, or make a standard invoice without GST.
  • Choose from six editable invoice designs and switch templates while preparing your invoice.
  • Add multiple products or services with quantity, rate and automatic line totals.
  • Enter business details, customer details, invoice number, date, payment status and notes.
  • Apply a discount and review the calculated subtotal, tax and final total.
  • Print or save the invoice as a PDF. No account or AI service is required.

How to Make an Invoice

  1. Select one of the invoice templates above.
  2. Enter your business and customer information.
  3. Add each product or service, its quantity and rate.
  4. Choose GST settings if applicable and check the total.
  5. Select Print / Save PDF to keep or share your invoice.

Who Can Use This Invoice Generator?

This online invoice generator can be used by freelancers, consultants, retailers, service providers and small businesses that need a simple invoice layout. Check the tax details and business information before issuing an invoice.
